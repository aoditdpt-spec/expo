      Questions

      Before you book

      The expo has already started. Can you still come?

      Often, yes. Send your stall number and the days you need, and we'll tell you honestly which crew is free and how soon they can be there.

      Can we book just one day, or a few hours?

      Yes. Pick only the days you need in the form and mention the time in "Brief for the artists". A launch or a VIP visit can be covered on its own.

      What do we need to arrange?

      Exhibitor or crew passes for our team, from you or the organiser, and a corner of the stall to keep a bag. We bring all the equipment.

      Who owns the photos and videos?

      They're yours to use for your brand, everywhere. If we'd like to show a few in AOD's own portfolio, we'll ask first.

      How do we pay?

      Pay the amount the AOD team shared with you by UPI, using the details in the form, and add the payment screenshot. Your booking is confirmed once we've checked the payment, and we tell you on WhatsApp.

      Are drones allowed inside expos?

      Only where the venue and organiser allow it. We check the rules for your expo before we promise any aerial shots.
