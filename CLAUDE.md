# CLAUDE.md

A single static page, "AOD for Exhibitors", for pitching and booking exhibiting brands at expos. The main offer is an expo reel: 1 reel and stall photos for ₹3,499 (shown after ~~₹5,000~~) (one shoot day per booking: the form's date chips are radios, so the amount is the price; the code still multiplies by the days picked if that ever changes; offer wording and the UPI payment details are in `config.js`; on phones the QR, UPI ID and Pay button open the UPI app with that amount through upi:// links). Expos so far: FIRSST 2026 and IIF 2026 (both Gandhinagar). With several expos on, the hero's "Booking now" line names them all and "Congratulations" says "at the expo"; a `?expo=<id>` link (each expo's QR in `qr/`) names that expo in the hero. The form's expo list always starts on "Choose the expo" (no default, no ?expo= preselection, not restored from the draft), as the owner asked. The owner wants it simple: hero with the offer, brand logos, the form, portfolio and a closing band (in that order). The longer pitch sections (why cover your stall, extra services, before/during/after, what you take home, how it works, the FAQ) were removed on purpose; don't add sections back without being asked. `README.md` explains the page and `sheetsguide.md` covers the Google Sheet setup; keep both in step with changes.

- **No framework, no build step.** Plain `index.html`, `assets/css/styles.css` and `assets/js/app.js`, one IIFE with no libraries. Keep it that way so it can be hosted anywhere and edited by non-developers. `npm run dev` runs `scripts/serve.mjs`, a dependency-free static server on port 3001 (3000 is the aod.co.in project's dev server).
- **Data and settings live outside the page:** expos in `data/expos.js` (`window.AOD_EXPOS`; the form lists expos on or coming up, then ones that ended within the last 30 days (`KEEP_AFTER_END_DAYS`), and every day of an expo can be picked, past days included, so shoots can be recorded afterwards; the hero's "Booking now" line uses only expos on or coming up; Indian time), the offer, payment, contact details and the Sheet address in `config.js`, and extra portfolio work in `data/portfolio.js`. Don't hard-code an expo, a price or a phone number in the HTML or JS.
- **Saving:** with `config.submitUrl` set, the form POSTs JSON as `text/plain`, so there's no CORS preflight, to the Apps Script web app in `google-apps-script/Code.gs`. The script appends a row to "All expo bookings" and a per-expo tab, saves the screenshot to Drive and emails the team. Show "Booking received" only after the script answers `{ ok: true }`. Without a `submitUrl`, or when saving fails, the booking goes to WhatsApp and the page says nothing was saved. When changing what the form sends, change `Code.gs` (its `HEADERS` and `row`) and the column table in `sheetsguide.md` to match.
- **Look:** same as aod.co.in: AOD orange `#ff6201`, white and black, through the tokens at the top of `styles.css`. Bricolage Grotesque for headings, Montserrat for text, no text shadows or glows. The page is phones first, since it's mostly shown on a phone at a stall: inputs at 16px or more and big touch targets.
- **Honesty, as on aod.co.in:** no made-up ratings, counts or client claims. The portfolio shows only real AOD work (the GATE 2026 coverage, real brand logos). The mock category photos in `assets/img/services/` are no longer used; never show them as AOD's work.
- **Hero:** no background video (removed on the owner's request); black with a fine orange dot grid. Three parts of the same height on laptops: the text on the left, a 9:16 reel in the middle (`.hero__reel`, from `heroVideo` in `config.js`: the GTZ stall reel, 720×1280 H.264 with GTZ's top-right watermark cropped out by trimming 13% off the top and the sides; muted with a sound button, loaded only after the page; a tap pauses or plays it; on laptops and desktops (`(min-width: 1024px) and (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`) the player frame (`.reel-frame`) moves to a small fixed player in the bottom-left corner once its box scrolls up out of view, with a ✕ that closes it for the visit; it doesn't pop up for someone who paused it; with an empty `src` it shows a "VIDEO HERE" box), and the offer card on the right; on phones they stack in that order.
- **Portfolio footage:** AOD's GATE 2026 coverage (the same files as aod.co.in's hero), zoomed 1.38× around a point a little above the middle, which crops out the "Ganesh Housing" and "Million Minds" logos at the top and a caption bar near the bottom. It plays muted only while on screen, and gets controls instead with reduced motion or Data Saver.
