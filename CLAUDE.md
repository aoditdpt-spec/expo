# CLAUDE.md

A single static page, "AOD for Exhibitors", for pitching and booking exhibiting brands at expos. The main offer is an expo reel: 1 reel and a few stall photos for ₹3,500. The owner wants it simple: hero with the offer, brand logos, portfolio, the form, a short FAQ and a closing band. The longer pitch sections (why cover your stall, extra services, before/during/after, what you take home, how it works) were removed on purpose; don't add sections back without being asked. `README.md` explains the page and `sheetsguide.md` covers the Google Sheet setup; keep both in step with changes.

- **No framework, no build step.** Plain `index.html`, `assets/css/styles.css` and `assets/js/app.js`, one IIFE with no libraries. Keep it that way so it can be hosted anywhere and edited by non-developers. `npm run dev` runs `scripts/serve.mjs`, a dependency-free static server on port 3001 (3000 is the aod.co.in project's dev server).
- **Data and settings live outside the page:** expos in `data/expos.js` (`window.AOD_EXPOS`; the form lists only expos whose last day hasn't passed, in Indian time), the offer, payment, contact details and the Sheet address in `config.js`, and extra portfolio work in `data/portfolio.js`. Don't hard-code an expo, a price or a phone number in the HTML or JS.
- **Saving:** with `config.submitUrl` set, the form POSTs JSON as `text/plain`, so there's no CORS preflight, to the Apps Script web app in `google-apps-script/Code.gs`. The script appends a row to "All expo bookings" and a per-expo tab, saves the screenshot to Drive and emails the team. Show "Booking received" only after the script answers `{ ok: true }`. Without a `submitUrl`, or when saving fails, the booking goes to WhatsApp and the page says nothing was saved. When changing what the form sends, change `Code.gs` (its `HEADERS` and `row`) and the column table in `sheetsguide.md` to match.
- **Look:** same as aod.co.in: AOD orange `#ff6201`, white and black, through the tokens at the top of `styles.css`. Bricolage Grotesque for headings, Montserrat for text, no text shadows or glows. The page is phones first, since it's mostly shown on a phone at a stall: inputs at 16px or more and big touch targets.
- **Honesty, as on aod.co.in:** no made-up ratings, counts or client claims. The portfolio shows only real AOD work (the GATE 2026 coverage, real brand logos). The mock category photos in `assets/img/services/` are no longer used; never show them as AOD's work.
- **Hero footage:** AOD's GATE 2026 coverage, the same files as aod.co.in's hero. It's zoomed 12% from the left on every screen but upright phones, to crop out the "Million Minds Tech City" logo. The video is only added after the page has loaded, and never on Data Saver or with reduced motion.
